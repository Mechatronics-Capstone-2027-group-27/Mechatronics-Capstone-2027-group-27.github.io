## What I worked on
General questions and conversation with Prof. Devaud regarding approach to glider design and analysis of aircraft performance.

- How to approach airfoil design?
- What tools to use to verify calculations?

## Results
Prof. Devaud agreed to be our advisor for the project

Main Conclusions:
- Use existing design literature, don't start from scratch
- Weight is an important consideration
- Use simplified calculations for gliders: toy gilders / RC gliders / glider websites are a good start
- A toy glider can make a good first prototype, especially for verifying calculations
- Find lift and drag coefficients for selected airfoil, use Newton's 2nd law to calculate drag force for sample glider prototype as first check of equations
- Separate rolling and pitching motions in the calculations
- The math is easy once you know what you have to do
- Don't do CFD except for verifying chosen design as a final test
