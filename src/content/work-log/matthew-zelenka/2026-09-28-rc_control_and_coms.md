## Work Completed

Researched different communication methods that could be used to stream telemetry and raw video from the aircraft to the ground.

The research focused on:
- LoRa and other sub-GHz radios for long-range telemetry.
- ELRS and MAVLink for transmitting aircraft telemetry.
- Wi-Fi for higher-bandwidth data and video streaming.
- Analog 5.8 GHz systems for transmitting a raw live video feed.
- Different camera and video transmitter configurations for the video system.

Also reviewed example hardware and documentation to understand the available communication options and how they could be integrated into the aircraft.

## Results

The research identified several possible approaches for streaming aircraft telemetry and raw video. Lower-bandwidth radio systems such as LoRa and ELRS are being considered for telemetry, while Wi-Fi and analog FPV systems are being considered for video transmission.
